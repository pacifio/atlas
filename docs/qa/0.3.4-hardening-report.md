## 5. Recommendation

**Ship**, once the human smoke rows have been run. Every major that let an agent act outside its
rules or let the user approve something they could not see (U1, U2, U5, O3, U3, O1, O2) is fixed and
pinned by a test that fails without the fix. O1 and O2 were decided on 2026-09-27: the card is
prepared (Decline only) until it is described, and a message is approved one call at a time
(ADR-0014 amendment).

Before shipping:

1. Run the human smoke rows H6–H15 — none of the outward paths has had a live post yet, and the
   preparing card (O1) should be seen once on a real, slow-ish network.

C1 and L7 are fixed and verified in the running app (2026-09-27), along with O7 found while
testing them. Acceptable to ship with, tracked: U4, C2 (a ~300 ms row shift while a reloaded chat
streams, pinned) and the minors.
